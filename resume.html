Projects
Hearth
Creator
2026 · hearth-ledger-demo.fly.dev ↗

Household finance advisor that recommends what to do next with a household's money, not just where it went. Pulls live bank data through SimpleFIN, runs on Fly.io, and exposes ten read-only tools to Claude through its own connector. A per-person privacy model scopes every query: shared household accounts are visible to both partners, personal spending stays private. 800+ automated tests — and the bugs that mattered (doubled interest, a drifting mortgage balance, a broken autopay counter) surfaced from my own review of the screens, then went back into the build. I set the requirements, designed it, and tested it; Claude Code wrote the code (Python, FastAPI, SQLite).
